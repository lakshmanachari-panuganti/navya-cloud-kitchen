Our Story

A Kitchen Born
from Amma's Recipes

What started as weekend batches for friends and family grew into something beautiful. Every powder is hand-roasted, every laddu hand-rolled — just like how our mothers and grandmothers made them.

No factory lines. No machines. No shortcuts. Just the patient art of traditional home cooking, done the way it's been done for generations.
