Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

We collect only what we need to fulfil your order: your name, WhatsApp number, and a delivery address. That's it.

What we do with it:

  • Reach you on WhatsApp to confirm the order and share delivery updates.
  • Hand your address to our delivery partner for the trip to your door.
  • Keep a simple record of past orders so we remember your preferences.

What we don't do:

  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone outside our delivery partner.
  • We don't run ads or track you across other websites.
  • We don't store payment card details - payment (when enabled) is handled directly by the gateway.

To have your data removed, message us on WhatsApp at +91 86862 16633 and we'll do it the same day.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: July 2026

By placing an order with Navya Cloud Kitchen Online, you agree to these terms:

  • Every item is handmade fresh to order. We prepare after your order is confirmed, so expect 24–48 hours between order and dispatch.
  • Prices shown are in Indian Rupees (INR) and are inclusive of all applicable charges on the food itself. There are no hidden fees on the items.
  • Courier charges are separate and paid directly to the delivery partner (Rapido / Porter / similar) at drop-off. Typical range within Hyderabad is ₹150–300 depending on distance and weight.
  • Orders close daily at 6 PM. Anything placed after that ships the next working day.
  • We can cancel or refuse an order if we're unable to fulfil it (rare - usually only when a key ingredient runs out). You'll be notified within a few hours and refunded in full.
  • You must be 18+ to place an order that requires payment.

We reserve the right to update these terms; the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Since every item is a fresh, handmade food product, we cannot accept returns once an order has been delivered.

We do stand behind quality. If you find any of the following, we'll make it right:

  • Wrong item or missing item - free replacement or full refund.
  • Quality issue (freshness, packaging damage, or taste concern) - reported to us via WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery, with a photo. We'll replace the item on the next delivery run or refund the item's price.
  • Order cancelled before preparation begins - full refund, no questions.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Please note: taste is subjective. We can't refund an item simply because it wasn't to your liking, but do tell us - we'll take it as feedback.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Navya Cloud Kitchen Online is a small home kitchen - we focus on the food, not on running a delivery fleet. Every order ships via a third-party courier partner (Rapido, Porter, or similar), and courier charges are paid directly to the courier at drop-off, not through this site.

  • Courier charges (customer pays): typically ₹150–₹300 within Hyderabad, depending on distance and package weight. The courier quotes the exact amount at pickup.
  • Preparation time: 24–48 hours from order confirmation, since every item is made fresh in small batches.
  • Dispatch: once the order is ready, we WhatsApp you the courier pickup details. You can either book Rapido / Porter yourself from our address, or we can help arrange the booking on your behalf - either way, the courier bill is settled at drop-off.
  • Coverage: Hyderabad and surrounding areas within Rapido/Porter's operating zone. For requests outside Hyderabad, message us on WhatsApp and we'll quote a reputable courier (allow 3–5 working days).
  • Order tracking: because the courier is booked in your name at pickup, tracking happens in the Rapido / Porter app on your phone. We keep you informed on WhatsApp too.

This model keeps our food prices low and honest - you pay only for the food + the actual courier cost, with nothing padded in between.

Questions? Message us anytime on WhatsApp.
