## 1. Sankranti (January 13–16)

### Products
- **Nuvvula Undalu** — hero product for the season
- **Bellam Palli Undalu** — peanut + jaggery, classic Sankranti sweet
- **Sankranti Combo Box** — 250g each of both undalu in festive packaging

- **Jan 1:** Open pre-orders for Sankranti boxes

## General Marketing Principles

1. **Start 2–3 weeks early** — People plan festival shopping ahead
2. **Use Telugu text** — Builds trust with the target audience
3. **Show freshness** — BTS videos of preparation the night before
4. **Create urgency** — Fixed cutoff dates, limited slots
5. **Social proof** — Share order counts ("50+ families ordered!")
6. **Repeat the core message** — Homemade, fresh, no preservatives, made to order
7. **Simple CTA** — Reply with a keyword or click a link
