## Brand-Voice Guardrails

Every caption, hashtag, and image is FSSAI-aware. We stay in heritage / flavour / occasion / usage territory and **avoid all health claims**.

| ✅ Safe | ❌ Avoid |
|---|---|
| Traditional, heritage, grandmother's recipe | "Boosts immunity", "aids digestion", "hair growth" |
| Nutty, smoky, rich, earthy, warm | "Superfood", "detox", "cleanse", "cure" |
| Slow-roasted, hand-ground, small-batch | "Medicinal", "therapeutic", "prevents disease" |
| Festival, seasonal, weekly-stock, everyday | Any specific health outcome |
| Nutrient-rich, wholesome (only if factual) | "Heart-healthy", "diabetic-friendly" without proof |

"Fresh, healthy & Traditional" is the only "healthy" word — implicit lifestyle, not a therapeutic claim.

# Compliance Guardrails (repeat here so nobody forgets)

Even in a comment reply, if you write "great for hair" or "boosts immunity", that's a health claim FSSAI can pull up. Push back gently in DMs with a safe alternative:

| If someone asks | Safe reply |
|---|---|
| "Is this good for diabetes?" | "It's a homemade traditional recipe — for anything specific to your health, please consult your doctor. Ingredients: [list]." |
| "Will this help my hair grow?" | "Curry leaves are a treasured South Indian ingredient — I can't promise health outcomes, but the flavour speaks for itself 🌿" |
| "Is this a superfood?" | Reply avoiding the word: "It's a wholesome traditional podi made with [ingredients]." |
